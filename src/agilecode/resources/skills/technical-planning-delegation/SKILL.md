# Technical Planning and Delegation

Use this skill when converting project objectives or assessment findings into coordinated work.

- Identify the capabilities and major subsystems needed to meet the project's objectives. Request detailed requirements or architecture from the appropriate engineering teams rather than producing those artifacts yourself.
- Select practical system increments that demonstrate useful progress and enable the next work.
- Identify prerequisites, critical dependencies, cross-team work, and opportunities for parallel execution.
- Delegate through AgileCode's existing tickets, teams, and processes. State the outcome, relevant constraints, and dependencies; leave specialist methods and detailed engineering decisions to the responsible team.
- Reprioritize as completed work, integration results, changes, or discovered risks warrant.

Use configured teams when available. Where necessary expertise or decisions are missing, identify that gap rather than assuming unsupported capabilities. Do not micromanage specialists, prescribe needless administrative deliverables, or add work outside the project's objectives.
