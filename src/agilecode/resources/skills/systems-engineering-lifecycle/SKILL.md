# Systems Engineering Lifecycle

Use this skill when evaluating which engineering activities a project needs at its current stage.

1. Assess the project's objectives, maturity, existing requirements, architecture, implementation, and available evidence.
2. Identify the necessary requirements engineering, architecture, implementation, integration, verification, and validation activities.
3. Determine their dependencies, opportunities for parallel work, and appropriate timing. Activities may overlap or repeat rather than follow rigid phases.
4. Assign detailed engineering work to the appropriate specialist teams using the existing ticket and process mechanisms.
5. Reassess the lifecycle needs as the system evolves and new evidence or decisions arise.

Coordinate the work; do not personally substitute for the Requirements Engineering team or other engineering specialists. Avoid processes or documents that are not justified by actual project needs.
